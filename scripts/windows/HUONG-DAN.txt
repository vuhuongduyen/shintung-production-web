ONSPA - DONG BO SOURCE VA SAO LUU DAY DU - WINDOWS
1. Giai nen 5 file vao C:\website onspa (giu cac file cung folder).
2. Tren web: Admin -> Sao luu & Ban giao -> Dong bo source va sao luu day du.
3. Nhap ten may, bam Tao ma sao luu day du -> Sao chep ma.
4. Nhan doi Cai-dong-bo.cmd, dan ma va Enter. Bo cai cu can chay lai ban nay.
5. Kiem tra thong bao SAO LUU DAY DU THANH CONG. Ban trong backups phai co HOAN-TAT.json.

Source: C:\website onspa\source. Tu kiem tra moi 15 phut.
Sao luu DB, tat ca file R2 va source: C:\website onspa\backups.
Tu tao 1 ban moi moi ngay khi dang nhap Windows va co mang. Neu may tat thi lam khi mo lai.
Sao-luu-ngay.cmd: tao them ban sao luu day du ngay.
Dong-bo-ngay.cmd: kiem tra source va sao luu neu hom nay chua co ban.
Nhat ky: C:\website onspa\_sync\dong-bo.log.
Ban source cu: C:\website onspa\versions. Khong tu xoa ban cu hay ban sao luu.
Ma chi source cu khong duoc doc DB/files. Can Tao ma sao luu day du va cai lai.

Ma het han, doi mat khau, thu hoi, khoa tai khoan: tao ma moi va cai lai.
Dung tu dong: Windows+R -> shell:startup -> xoa shortcut ONSPA-source-sync-, dang xuat Windows.
Khong can quyen Administrator. Khong can Git hay kho ben ngoai.
